Sojourn of a bivariate Gaussian field above a high level

A stationary Gaussian field X(t) = (X1(t), X2(t)) on the square [0,T]2, with cross-correlation ρ between the two components. The sojourn set is where both coordinates exceed the level u; its area ∫ 1{X(t) > u b} dt is the quantity whose tail is studied in Dębicki, Hashorva & Michna (2025). Raise u and watch the sojourn set collapse into a few small islands.

—
Both components are built from the same random Fourier features, so the field is exactly stationary. The bottom strip shows how the area decays as the level grows — the log-scale curve is what the asymptotics in the paper describe.
