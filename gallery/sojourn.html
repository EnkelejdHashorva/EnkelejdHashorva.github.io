Sojourn of a bivariate Gaussian field above a high level

A centred, unit-variance stationary Gaussian field X(t) = (X1(t), X2(t)) on the unit square [0,1]2. Each component has covariance r(h) = exp(−(h₁²+h₂²)/(2ℓ²)), and Cov(X1(s),X2(t)) = ρ r(t−s). The sojourn set is where both coordinates exceed the level u; its area is Lu = ∫[0,1]² 1{X1(t) > u, X2(t) > u} dt. Tail probabilities of rescaled sojourn areas are studied in Dębicki, Hashorva & Michna (arXiv:2510.06930). Here the threshold vector is b = (1,1). Raise u to see the excursion set of this one realisation shrink.

Expected area across fields: —
Both field panels share the scale ≤ −3 ≥ 3 (blue = 0). Pale green marks simultaneous exceedance in the third panel. The 120×120 grid is sampled from the Gaussian covariance by numerical factorisation. We use X₁ = A and X₂ = ρA + √(1−ρ²)B with independent copies A and B; changing ρ keeps these copies fixed. The displayed area is a grid approximation and can miss small excursions. The bottom strip uses a logarithmic area axis and ends at the last sampled level with positive area. The solid blue trace shows this realisation. The dashed orange curve shows the exact mean area E Lᵤ = P(Z₁ > u, Z₂ > u) for two standard normals of correlation ρ, evaluated by quadrature. Stationarity and the square’s unit area reduce the expectation to the probability at one point, so it is independent of ℓ. The plot shows areas down to one cell. The paper studies probabilities across random realisations, P{θ(u)Lᵤ > x}, as u → ∞; this curve is not such a probability.
