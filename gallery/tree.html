A branch-stationary max-stable field on a binary tree

Vertices are words over the alphabet {L, R}. Along each edge an independent Gaussian increment with variance Δ is added, giving a branching random walk W. The field is ηv = maxi Pi exp(Wv(i) − Δ|v|/2), with Pi the points of a Poisson process. Every subtree looks, in law, like the whole tree — that is branch-stationarity. From Hashorva & Novikov (2026).

Small Δ: the same Poisson point tends to win everywhere (one colour). Large Δ: the winning point changes from branch to branch — the field fragments into independent-looking patches, as the branching random walk spreads out.
