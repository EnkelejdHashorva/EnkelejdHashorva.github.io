Simultaneous exceedance of a bivariate Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process

The stationary companion of the quadrant animation. Two Ornstein–Uhlenbeck processes with covariance e−|t| and cross-correlation ρ run over [0, T]; the rare event is that at some time both exceed their levels, X₁(t) > u and X₂(t) > b₂u. Which coordinates are “essential” is decided by the quadratic programme min x⊤Σ−1x over x ≥ (1, b₂): for ρ < b₂ both are, and at the moment of entry X₂ sits right on its threshold; for ρ ≥ b₂ only X₁ is, and X₂ enters with slack ≈ (ρ − b₂)u to spare. Unlike the Brownian case, the entry time is uniform on [0,T] — stationarity — so the dimension reduction shows in where the path enters, not when. The exceedance probability is asymptotically T ℋ u2 P(X(0) > u b) with a multidimensional Pickands constant ℋ. From Dębicki, Hashorva & Wang, Extremes of vector-valued Gaussian processes.

Exact simulation of the stationary process (AR(1) step with e−dt, dt = 0.01, T = 6, stationary start). Paths are found by brute force, up to 60 000 attempts.
