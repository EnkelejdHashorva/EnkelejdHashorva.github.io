Simultaneous exceedance of a bivariate Ornstein–Uhlenbeck process

The stationary companion of the quadrant animation. Two Ornstein–Uhlenbeck processes with covariance matrix R(t) = e−|t|Σ, where Σ has unit diagonal and off-diagonal entries ρ, run over [0, T]. The rare event is a simultaneous exceedance of u and b₂u. The quadratic programme min x⊤Σ−1x over x ≥ (1, b₂) identifies three cases. For ρ < b₂, both coordinates are essential and high-level entry concentrates near the corner. For ρ > b₂, only the first is essential, with second-coordinate slack (ρ − b₂)u + OP(1). At ρ = b₂, that slack is of order one and the second constraint still affects the leading constant. Conditional on an exceedance, the first entry time becomes uniform on [0,T] as u → ∞; it is not exactly uniform at finite levels. The high-level exceedance probability is asymptotically T ℋ u2 P(X(0) > u b) with the appropriate multidimensional Pickands constant ℋ. From Dębicki, Hashorva & Wang, Extremes of vector-valued Gaussian processes, Theorem 2.1. The uniform-time limit follows by applying the theorem to each initial subinterval.

Exact stationary OU samples on a grid (AR(1) coefficient e−dt, dt = 0.01, T = 6). A stationary start already above both levels counts as time-0 entry. Crossings between samples are missed, and detected entries can overshoot.
